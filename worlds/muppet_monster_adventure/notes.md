- 4/4 ghoul-friend amulets

Ghoul-friend:

- 288/320 evil energy
- 3 tokens
- B_N_S

Ghoul-friend + Werebear:

- 317/320 evil energy (+29)
- 4/5 tokens (+1)
- BON_S (+O)

Ghoul-friend + Werebear + Noseferatu:

- 320/320 evil energy (+3)
- 5/5 tokens (+1)
- BONUS (+U)

These are actually possible to do without Noseferatu, but I'm going to consider it a trick since it's clearly not intended.
